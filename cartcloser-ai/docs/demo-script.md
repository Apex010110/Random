# Live demo script (2–4 minutes, four presenters)

**Setup (before going on stage)**
1. Open the app on a laptop browser (web build) or Android phone (APK). Both run **offline**; no Wi-Fi needed.
2. Welcome → **Guided judge demo**. Press the reset icon (↻) once so counters start at zero.
3. Backup: a second device with the same build open on the welcome screen (offline). Keep the pitch-deck preview video ready too.
4. Operator: one person clicks **Do this step**. Speakers never touch the laptop.

| Time | Speaker | Click(s) | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Member 1 (lead) | Step 1 | "Online stores pay to bring shoppers in. Many leave at checkout with one unanswered question. CartCloser AI answers it, with the shopper's permission." |
| 0:25–1:45 | Member 2 (product) | Steps 2–5 | "Ayesha opens the linen kurta, adds size M and checks out. The WhatsApp reminder box is optional and unticked by default; she ticks it herself. Then she leaves." |
| | | Steps 6–8 | "The merchant sees one eligible abandoned cart. In demo mode we skip the 20-minute wait, but consent, age and quiet-hours checks still run. Ayesha gets one approved message that tells her how to opt out." |
| | | Steps 9–10 | "She asks 'Will it arrive in Karachi by Friday?' The assistant shows the store's estimated window and says honestly that it can't guarantee Friday. 'COD hai?' works in Roman Urdu or Urdu too." |
| | | Steps 11–12 | "The signed link takes her back to her saved cart; she confirms cash on delivery." |
| 1:45–2:25 | Member 3 (market) | Step 13 | "The dashboard updated live: one recovered order and PKR 4,690 of attributed sales, gross, not profit. Our paying customers are small and mid-sized Shopify and WooCommerce merchants; their shoppers include urban teens, young adults and adults, and outbound reminders are blocked for under-18s." |
| | | Scenario B (optional) | "If a shopper doesn't opt in, nothing is sent. She can still use on-site chat." |
| 2:25–3:10 | Member 4 (commercial) | Merchant → Campaign / Integrations | "Merchants set the delay, quiet hours, contact cap and the discount ceiling. Shopify, WooCommerce and WhatsApp show *Not connected*, honestly; next we pilot with 5–10 consenting stores and measure recovery against a holdout group. Thank you." |

**If something goes wrong:** press Back/Next to re-run a step (each step sets up its own prerequisites), or reset and pick the scenario again. In offline mode no network failure can affect the demo.

**Do not claim:** live WhatsApp sending, Shopify/WooCommerce integrations, Meta approval, real merchants, or real recovery rates.
