## Slide 1: CartCloser AI · [Member 1] · ~40 s
*On slide entry, the glow, logo, name and tagline animate in automatically (~2 s). Wait for them.*

> "Online stores pay to bring shoppers in. So what happens when those shoppers leave at checkout?"
>
> "Good [morning/afternoon]. We are CartCloser AI."

**[CLICK 1]** The cart, chat and check nodes draw along the path (~3 s).
> "A shopper fills a cart, gets stuck on one question (will it fit, when will it arrive, can I pay cash) and leaves. Most stores answer with a one-way reminder. We want to start a helpful, permission-based conversation instead, and turn it into a completed order."

**[CLICK 2]** The vision card fades up.
> "Our vision is simple: every online shopper gets the help they need to finish buying. [Member 2] will show you how it works."

## Slide 2: From abandoned cart to completed order · [Member 2] · ~60 s
*The headline and an empty phone are on screen.*

**[CLICK 1]** Step 01 appears.
> "It starts when a shopper leaves checkout with a question still unanswered."

**[CLICK 2]** Step 02, then the chat plays: opt-in chip, greeting, shopper question, typing dots, AI reply (~4 s).
> "If that shopper has opted in to WhatsApp messages, CartCloser AI sends one approved message. When they reply, the AI answers in Urdu or English, using only the store's own information: size guides, delivery estimates and payment options."

**[CLICK 3]** The COD question and the checkout link appear.
> "If the store offers cash on delivery for that item, the AI says so and sends a one-tap link back to checkout. If a question is unclear, it goes to a human. The AI never invents prices, stock or delivery promises."

**[CLICK 4]** Step 03 and the green confirmation appear.
> "The shopper checks out by link or with a supported COD confirmation. Any discount stays within limits the merchant sets."

**[CLICK 5]** The merchant note and outcome line appear.
> "Merchants control the messages, the discounts and the opt-out. No opt-in, no WhatsApp message. Helpful answers, less friction, more completed orders. [Member 3] will explain why we built this."

*The phone chat is labelled "Illustrative demo · Concept". Do not describe it as a live integration.*

## Slide 3: The sale shouldn't end at a question · [Member 3] then [Member 4]

**[Member 3] · ~40 s**

**[CLICK 1]** The three concern cards appear.
> "When a sale is lost at checkout, it is often a question nobody answered. Will it fit? When will it arrive? Is cash on delivery available?"

**[CLICK 2]** The concerns flow into the AI and come out as answers.
> "CartCloser AI turns those concerns into answers from the store's own size guide, delivery estimate and payment options, then gives the shopper a path back to checkout. Our paying customers are small and mid-sized Shopify and WooCommerce merchants and their agencies. The shoppers they serve are urban teens, young adults and adults. For under-18s we use guardian-consent safeguards or leave them out of outbound marketing, and we never target children under 13."

**[Member 4] · ~30 s**

**[CLICK 3]** The closing statement and value chips appear.
> "We help merchants recover opportunities from shoppers they've already attracted. That means less checkout friction, a design built around local habits like COD, and potentially fewer failed deliveries and returns, which we will measure in pilots rather than assume. Next we plan to build the MVP and test it with 5 to 10 consenting pilot stores, measuring opt-in, replies, recovered orders and opt-outs against a holdout group. We'd value your feedback and any introductions to merchants who want to pilot with us. Thank you."
