# CartCloser AI: Investor / Judge Q&A Prep

Keep each answer to 20–30 seconds. Any number below is a **planning estimate or hypothesis**, not a validated result. Say so when you use one.

**1. Why WhatsApp?**
In Pakistan and similar mobile-first markets, many shoppers already talk to stores on WhatsApp. It supports two-way conversation, so we can answer the question that stopped the sale. An email reminder is one-way.

**2. Why would shoppers opt in?**
Because they get something useful in return: order updates, delivery answers and help with their order. The opt-in is a clear, unticked checkbox at checkout explaining what they will receive. We will measure the opt-in rate in pilots.

**3. What if they decline?**
Then we send no WhatsApp message at all. They can still use on-site chat. Giving a phone number at checkout is not consent to marketing, and we never bypass consent.

**4. What is unique compared with existing apps?**
Related tools exist (Shopify emails, Klaviyo/Omnisend flows, WhatsApp marketing platforms). Our planned edge is the combination of local COD workflows, Urdu/Roman Urdu, answers grounded in store policy, merchant-set discount limits and transparent attribution. We still need to validate it in pilots, and we do not claim to be the only tool.

**5. How does COD confirmation work?**
Where the store's system supports it, the shopper confirms their cash-on-delivery order in the chat, and the confirmation goes back to the store. The aim is to cut fake or uncertain COD orders before they are shipped. Courier integration is planned for later, not built yet.

**6. What does the merchant pay?**
Proposed: a free trial or low-volume Starter plan, then a subscription plus a transparent usage fee. An earlier draft assumed $79/month + 2.5% of attributed recovered sales. That is a hypothesis to test, not a final price.

**7. How is teen privacy protected?**
Teens (13–19) are shoppers, not our paying customers. For under-18s we require guardian consent or exclude them from outbound marketing, and we use no pressure-based offers. We never target children under 13. We collect minimal data, encrypt it and provide deletion controls.

**8. What is the MVP?**
One platform (Shopify or WooCommerce), English text replies, a secure checkout-recovery link and a merchant dashboard. Urdu, COD/courier integration and voice notes come later, based on demand.

**9. What is the pilot metric?**
Recovered orders against a holdout group of shoppers who get no message. We also track opt-in rate, reply rate, opt-out rate, discount cost and margin impact. Logistics metrics are failed-delivery rate and return rate.

**10. What about wrong AI answers?**
The AI answers only from merchant-approved information: catalogue, policies and supported shipping data. It never invents prices, stock, refunds or delivery promises. Uncertain questions go to a human, and every conversation is logged for merchant review.

**11. How do you calculate recovered revenue?**
We compare a messaged group with a holdout group. Recovered revenue is the extra orders the messaged group placed, not every order that followed a message.
*Hypothetical:* $10,000/month of eligible abandoned checkouts × 3% / 5% / 8% recovery = $300 / $500 / $800 recovered sales. At a 30% margin that is $90 / $150 / $240 contribution, before discounts and fees. Recovered sales are not profit.

**12. How do you avoid spam?**
Opted-in shoppers only. We send one approved template after a wait (default 30 minutes) and follow up only if the shopper replies. Every message has an easy STOP opt-out and a frequency cap. We follow WhatsApp Business Platform policy and local privacy law.

**13. What about courier integration?**
It is on the roadmap for months 12–24, prioritised by pilot demand. In the MVP, delivery answers come from the store's published delivery estimates.

**Bonus: What does it cost to build?**
The planning estimate is about $4,100 one-time: MVP $2,500, registration/legal $300, pilot and launch marketing $1,200, platform/API setup $100. Running costs are about $150/month fixed, plus per-conversation AI and WhatsApp fees (Meta pricing to be confirmed).

**Bonus: What traction do you have?**
None yet, and we won't claim any. Our next steps are an interactive demo, merchant interviews, then 5–10 consenting pilot stores.
